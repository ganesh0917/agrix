Common Farming Challenges

Find help with major problems faced by farmers. Click "Contact" on any issue to get professional assistance.

Ventilation Problems

Inadequate ventilation in storage facilities leads to moisture buildup, mold growth, and crop deterioration. This causes significant losses in agricultural products.

Delay in Transportation

Poor logistics and transportation delays result in spoilage of perishable goods. Extended transit times increase the risk of product quality degradation and financial losses.

Materials Spoiled

Crop spoilage leads to significant wastage and reduced profit margins. Proper storage conditions and handling practices are essential to minimize losses.

Pest & Disease Infestation

Pest infestations and diseases in storage facilities can destroy entire harvests. Early detection and proper control measures are crucial for crop protection.

Cold Chain Management

Inadequate temperature control during storage and transport compromises product quality. Maintaining proper cold chain is essential for perishables.

Market Price Fluctuation

Unpredictable market prices affect farming profitability. Understanding market trends and price forecasting helps optimize selling strategies.
